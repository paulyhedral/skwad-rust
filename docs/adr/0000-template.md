# ADR-0000: Template

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem needs a decision? Keep it to the facts a
future reader needs to understand why this came up.

## Decision

The change being made, stated in the present tense: "We use X for Y."

## Options considered

- **Option A** - what it is, why it was or was not chosen.
- **Option B** - same.
- Include the rejected options. An ADR with one option is a note, not a decision.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what new constraints this imposes, and
what has to be revisited if the decision is reversed.
